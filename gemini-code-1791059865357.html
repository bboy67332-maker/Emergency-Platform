What to say when you call

Stay calm and follow this standard sequence to get help as fast as possible:

  1. 1
    State what happened clearly (accident, crime, medical emergency, fire).
  2. 2
    Give your exact location, street name, and an easily recognized landmark.
  3. 3
    State how many people are hurt, trapped, or in danger.
  4. 4
    Provide your full name and an active contact number.
  5. 5
    Stay on the line until the operator explicitly instructs you to hang up.
Do not call emergency lines for non-emergencies. It delays help for people in danger.
